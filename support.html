Common questions

I don’t get any reminders

In Frogg, tap the gear on Today, then Reminders › Allow notifications. If notifications are off, the same place opens your iPhone’s Settings. Also check that a Focus isn’t silencing Frogg.

Can I tick a task from a reminder?

Yes. Touch and hold the reminder, then tap Done.

When does Frogg ask for my frog?

Settings › Daily plan: in the morning or the evening before, at the time you choose.

My tasks are gone

They’re saved on your iPhone and follow its backups (iCloud or computer). Reinstalling the app without a backup starts from scratch.

I bought Frogg Pro on another device

Open Settings › Frogg Pro › Restore purchases, with the same Apple Account.

How does the free trial work?

You get Frogg Pro free for 7 days. It ends on its own: nothing to cancel, nothing charged. Then the Pro features lock again and the free version keeps working.

How do I add the widget?

Widgets come with Frogg Pro. Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Frogg. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, then tap Customize.

How do I change the language?

Settings › Language opens Frogg’s page in your iPhone’s Settings. Frogg speaks English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Hindi and Arabic.
