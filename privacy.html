Privacy

Last updated: November 10, 2026. Publisher: Bruno Pennes (individual). Contact: bpennes@gmail.com.

Frogg collects no data

Your tasks, daily plans, settings, points, levels, badges and history are stored only on your iPhone. Frogg has no account and no server: we receive none of this data, and no one else does through us.

Widgets and reminders

The widgets read a copy of your day kept on your iPhone, shared only between the app and its widgets. Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone itself: their text never leaves it.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

Frogg shows no ads, doesn’t track you and contains no analytics or third-party code. It doesn’t ask for Apple’s tracking permission, because it has nothing to track.

Frogg Pro and the free trial

Purchases go through Apple’s App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether Frogg Pro or the trial is active.

Backups and crash reports

Like other app data, Frogg’s data is included in your iPhone’s backups (iCloud or computer), under your control. If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

Your rights

As we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. To erase everything, delete the app from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with a new date.
